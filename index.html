01 · About

Organized, tech-savvy and clear in how I communicate.

I help teams run smoothly by taking care of the details. Over the past three years I've worked across technical support, software engineering and data roles, which gave me a strong mix of people skills and technical skills.

I'm comfortable with email and calendar management, data entry and spreadsheets, online research, customer support and CRM and data management, using tools like Google Workspace, Microsoft Office, Salesforce, Slack, Teams, Zoom and Canva. I've worked fully remote alongside cross-functional teams, so I'm used to working independently and managing my own time.

I use AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini and Microsoft Copilot) to work faster and smarter: drafting and polishing emails and documents, researching and summarizing information, cleaning and organizing spreadsheet data, and building websites and automating repetitive tasks. I always review AI output for accuracy before it goes out.

I also build websites. I created a complete WordPress website for author Jeannie Roberts, with book pages, an online shop, and newsletter and ARC sign-ups.

I hold a BS in Computer Science from the University of St. La Salle and an EF SET English certificate at C1 Advanced level. Outside work I enjoy content creation, PC hardware and gaming technology.
