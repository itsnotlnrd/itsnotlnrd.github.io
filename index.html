01 · About

Analytical by nature, customer-focused by practice.

I'm a Technical Support Engineer at NiCE, where I investigate and resolve technical issues for customers. My work sits where deep technical analysis meets clear communication: understanding what's really going wrong, fixing it, and making sure the customer understands what happened.

Before support, I worked as a software engineer, database analyst and database administrator, handling ETL processes, data integrations, back-end issues and database security. I hold a BS in Computer Science from the University of St. La Salle. That hands-on engineering background helps me get to root causes faster.

Outside work I'm into game development, PC hardware, gaming technology and content creation. I'm looking to keep growing in technical roles where rigorous problem-solving and strong communication make a real difference.
